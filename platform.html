Transform How People Learn Through Conversations

Why Multiple Avatars?

With one avatar, you get a monologue. They explain, describe, tell. But real situations unfold through conversations. When two or more avatars interact naturally, everything changes.

Now your learners experience:

  • How situations really unfold in your world
  • The impact of their decisions playing out in rich dialogue
  • Skilled people handling pivotal situations
  • Different stakeholders' perspectives colliding and aligning

Learning with a Virtual Colleague

Imagine working with a colleague through a challenging situation. That's how AliveSim engages learners. The virtual colleague can:

  • Make mistakes that need guidance: "Oh wow, what do we do now?"
  • Have revelations: "I hadn't considered that perspective..."
  • Voice uncertainty: "I don't think this is the right time..."

Through this unique partnership, their colleague drives real situations while learners make the strategic decisions. Each needs the other to move forward in the simulation – creating a natural dynamic that elevates thinking from tactical to strategic.

Choose the Right Technology to Complete the Learning Journey

Organizations need specialized technology to complete the learning journey. With knowledge acquisition as Stage 1, learners then need to learn how to apply it—what to do in realistic situations. That's Stage 2. Sometimes, they also need to practice how to say it. That's Stage 3.

Selection Criteria Guided Learning Freeform Practice
Best Use Cases Applying knowledge, decision-making, learning what to do (Stage 2) Conversation practice, learning how to say it (Stage 3)
Engagement Approach Multiple interactive avatars, game engine responsiveness Conversation with one AI avatar
Development Approach AI-enhanced content creation, locked before delivery for reliability Standard conversation scenarios with basic company context options
Company-Specific Content Deep customization to address company-specific situations, without cost impact Limited company context; significant cost to add more, if technically feasible
Learner Experience Consistency 100% reliable content, same high-quality experience for all learners Different conversation each time; 10-20% chance of inaccurate responses
Cost Model Lower costs, predictable volume-based pricing (no AI processing fees) Higher costs, variable, driven by AI usage and processing fees
Performance Analytics Tracks decision-making mapped to learning objectives Conversation style and interaction analysis

The Limitations of Traditional E-Learning Simulations

E-learning platforms, like Articulate, Captivate, and iSpring excel at structured knowledge delivery. But their simulation capabilities face key limitations:

  • Limited learner engagement
  • Limited immersion
  • Minimal dynamic feedback
  • Limited decision analytics
  • Complex development

That's why organizations complement e-learning tools with specialized platforms for applying and reinforcing knowledge. This proven combination drives measurable results.
